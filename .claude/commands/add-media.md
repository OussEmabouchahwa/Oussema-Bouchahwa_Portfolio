---
description: Add images or videos to the portfolio
---
1. Ask which slot (see "Media slots" in docs/CONTENT.md) or read the file names I give you.
2. Compress: images to WebP or JPG under 250 KB at the slot size; video to H.264 1080p under 15 MB plus a JPG poster frame.
3. Save under `public/assets/` with the exact slot path.
4. `npm run build`, open the page, confirm the slot shows the media; run `media.spec.ts`.
